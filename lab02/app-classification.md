Biggest difference we found: The ABS controller operates under strict safety and real-time timing constraints (embedded control loop) where a missed deadline can cause physical harm, whereas consumer applications like Telegram and Moodle prioritize network connectivity, user availability, and data throughput over microsecond-level hardware execution.
