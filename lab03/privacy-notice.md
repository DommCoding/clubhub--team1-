# ITC Club Hub Privacy Notice

## Who we are
We are Clubhub Team1. We help students register for events and manage club memberships.

## What we collect and why

We collect your student ID and name to identify you and prevent duplicate registrations. We use your email for account login and registration confirmations. Email reminders are optional. We collect your phone number only if you agree to receive SMS reminders 24 hours before an event. We store club membership information to manage club member lists and use attendance records to create anonymous activity reports. The gender survey is voluntary, and we only keep aggregated statistics.

## Who sees it

You can view your own personal information. Club leaders can view names and relevant membership information. Organisers can view names and masked student IDs for event check-in. Administrators can access information for support and audits. Phone numbers are used by the automated messaging system and shared only with the SMS gateway provider.

## How long

We keep student IDs, names and email addresses until six months after graduation or account deletion. Membership information is deleted when you leave a club or six months after graduation. Phone numbers are deleted immediately when you withdraw consent or six months after graduation. Attendance records are anonymised within 30 days after an event, and the original records are deleted. 

## Your choices

Email reminders are turned off by default. SMS reminders and the gender survey are voluntary. You can decline these optional choices and still create an account. You can withdraw your SMS consent later through My Profile.

## Contact

If you have questions about your personal information, please Email us at `privacy@itcclubhub.edu`, which is responsible for managing your data.
