# Licence decision · ITC Club Hub
Version 1.0 · 2026-11-22 · Lead: <Keo Maknioddom>

| Criterion | MIT | Apache-2.0 | GPL-3.0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anyone (incl. companies) may reuse | Yes, with attribution | Yes, with attribution & notice | Yes, under GPL-3.0 terms |
| Improvements must be shared back | No | No | Yes, if distributed |
| Explicit patent grant | No (implied only) | Yes, plus patent retaliation clause | Yes, plus patent retaliation clause |
| Compatible with GoogleTest (BSD-3) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Simple for future student teams | Very simple and short | Moderate (notice & patent terms) | Strict copyleft rules to track |
| Instructor / institute requirement | Allowed (confirmed with TA) | Allowed (confirmed with TA) | Allowed (confirmed with TA) |

***Decision:*** MIT

***Rationale:*** We chose the MIT licence because it is short, clear, and easy for future ITC student teams to understand without heavy legal overhead. It permits broad reuse and modification with minimal conditions (keeping the copyright and permission notices intact), avoids strict copyleft distribution obligations, and is fully compatible with GoogleTest's BSD-3-Clause licence.
