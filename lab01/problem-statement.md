# Problem statement · ITC Club Hub (v0.1, 2026-10-20)

## Problem
The problem of fragmented event sign-ups and manual attendance tracking affects student clubs at ITC; the impact of which is overcrowded rooms and zero reliable records. A successful solution would centralize event discovery and automated check-ins.

## Vision
For ITC students and club leaders who struggle with disorganized chat-based sign-ups, ITC Club Hub is a centralized platform that unifies event management. Unlike informal group chats, our product enforces capacity limits and provides transparent oversight.

## Today's workaround
Clubs post event announcements in Telegram or Facebook chats and use physical paper sign-up sheets at the door.

## Scope
- Built in C++ this semester: Domain model, business rules, and CLI with file storage.
- Modelled only (web and mobile): Graphical user interfaces and push notifications.
- Out of scope: Payment gateways and external university timetable integrations.
