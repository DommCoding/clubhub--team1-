## Working agreements
1. Every pull request is reviewed by one other member within 24 hours on weekdays.
2. Anyone blocked for more than 2 hours posts in the team chat.
3. All code must compile with zero warnings before merging into the main branch.
4. Meeting agendas are posted at least 12 hours before any scheduled team sync.
5. Every team member must push at least one verified commit per lab task.

## Decision rules
- Product questions: Decided by the Product Owner after hearing team input.
- Technical questions: Reached by consensus within 24 hours, otherwise decided by majority vote.
- Deadlock (no agreement after 24 hours): Ask the TA at the next lab session.
- Where decisions are recorded: lab01/decisions.md
